Hi! I'm Joel Baker

In addition to specializing in trauma and helping with anxiety and depression, I work with a wide range of client concerns, including marriage and relationship difficulties, overwhelming emotions, parenting, and tough decision-making. Your therapy is tailored to your needs. That is whether you come on your own, with your family, or as a couple.

Experience

My experience spans hospital settings, family clinics, private practice, and working in clients' homes. I've supported at-risk teens and their families, provided leadership to teams of counselors, and supervise new therapists on their way to obtaining licensure. I have also served on the Georgia Association for Marriage and Family Therapy (GAMFT), working to further the health of our professional therapy community. These diverse experiences have shaped my approach, allowing me to help clients through what they bring to the room.

I primarily utilize Experiential Therapy as the foundation of my approach. Rather than just talking about your concerns, Experiential Therapy focuses on actively engaging with and working through challenges in a safe, professional setting. This client-centered method can create meaningful relief and empower you beyond the therapy room. While I am EMDR-trained and incorporate Acceptance and Commitment Therapy (ACT), Solution Focused Therapy, and other modalities, Experiential Therapy primarily guides our work together. I always tailor my approach to fit each client's unique needs, ensuring there is a thoughtful plan behind every session. My goal is to work alongside you, on your team, supporting your growth every step of the way.

Recent work

My recent work centers on supporting men's issues, strengthening couples relationships, providing affirming care as an LGBTQ+ ally, and helping clients heal from trauma. I am passionate about helping clients in all stages of life, including adults, teens, couples, and families.

I believe that everyone can find connection and fulfillment when they intentionally seek healing. My goal is to create a safe, confidential space where you can explore important choices and experiences to find direction and resolution. Therapy with me is a partnership, focused on growth, self-care, and building the life you want.
